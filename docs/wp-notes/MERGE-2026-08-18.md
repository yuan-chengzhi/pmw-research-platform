## Schema-version decision

WP-A and WP-B each added required fields to `launch.json` / `receipt.json` without
bumping `PMW_RUNTIME_LAUNCH_1` / receipt schema names; WP-B flagged the version call
for merge time. Decision: **keep the `_1` names.** These schemas are internal to an
unreleased control plane; no frozen contract governs them; the persisted acceptance
artifacts live in `pmw-research-data` under their own audit and are not re-validated
by this repo's store. Consequence, stated plainly: launch/receipt files written by
pre-merge builds are now rejected fail-closed (`MALFORMED_RUNTIME_LAUNCH` /
`MALFORMED_SESSION_RECEIPT`). That is the intended honesty semantics — those records
genuinely lack usage and verifier-kit epistemics — not an accident.
